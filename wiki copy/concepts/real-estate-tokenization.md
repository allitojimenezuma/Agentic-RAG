# Real estate tokenization

The process of converting real estate assets into digital tokens on a blockchain, representing ownership shares that can be traded. [[Álvaro Jiménez Martínez]] implemented this concept at [[UnknownGravity]], building a platform on the [[Polygon network]] that listed over €2.2 million in property assets. Technical implementation required careful synchronization of off-chain balances with on-chain token transactions using database semaphores and concurrency controls.

## Benefits

- Fractional ownership
- Increased liquidity for real estate assets
- 24/7 trading capability
- Reduced barriers to entry
